# Second Draft Workspace Redesign

## Intent

Second Draft is a dedicated rewriting desk, not a comparison screen and not a toolbar containing every editorial tool. The author writes the new version on the left and consults the protected old version on the right.

## Goals

- Make the next rewrite action obvious without obscuring the old draft.
- Keep the old draft read-only and visually secondary.
- Group secondary work (brief, passes, issues, memory and repetition aids) into one session surface.
- Preserve existing decisions, Send Ahead, source warnings, linked scroll, review passes and sealing.

## Non-goals

- Do not turn Second Draft into a diff viewer.
- Do not introduce automatic rewriting or AI-generated prose.
- Do not change the manuscript editor's save model.

## Chosen approach

Replace the right pane's equal-weight toolbar clusters with a rewrite desk: a compact source header, a visible queue/status rail, a focused decision dock and one Session drawer for secondary editorial tools. This retains all existing behavior while making one action at a time primary.
